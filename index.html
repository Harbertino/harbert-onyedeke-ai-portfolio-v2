06 / PROFILE & APPROACH

Bridging the gap between emerging technology and everyday people.

My work sits at the intersection of applied AI, education, entrepreneurship and community capacity development.

Through Embassey Group, I build digital products and learning initiatives that translate complex technology into accessible tools, workflows and practical experiences. My facilitation approach is demonstration-led: explain the concept, show the workflow, let participants practise, then evaluate the output and its limitations.

I am especially interested in responsible AI adoption, AI literacy for educators and young people, AI-enabled productivity, and locally relevant innovation across Nigeria and Africa.
