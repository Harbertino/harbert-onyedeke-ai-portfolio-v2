Onyedeke Harbert Izuchukwu

Also known professionally as Harbert Onyedeke and Facilitator Harbert.

Onyedeke Harbert Izuchukwu is a Nigerian AI Generalist, applied-AI product builder, generative AI educator and digital skills facilitator based in Oshodi, Lagos State. His work focuses on making emerging technology practical and accessible through AI education, applied product development, education technology and community capacity development.

As founder of Embassey Group, he leads initiatives spanning digital education, technology services, media and renewable energy. His professional approach combines explanation, live demonstration, guided practice and evaluation, with an emphasis on responsible AI use and locally relevant examples.
