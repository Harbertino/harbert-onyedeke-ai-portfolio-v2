PERSONAL BRAND IDENTITY

AI Chief Priest

The AI, innovation and digital transformation identity of Harbert Onyedeke.

AI Chief Priest is the public-facing personal brand identity used by Onyedeke Harbert Izuchukwu, also known as Harbert Onyedeke and Facilitator Harbert. The identity is associated with his work as a Nigerian AI Generalist, applied-AI product builder, generative AI educator and digital skills facilitator.

The brand communicates practical AI education, responsible adoption, product experimentation and the translation of emerging technologies into useful experiences for individuals, educators, young people and organisations.

Core themes

  • Artificial intelligence and generative AI education
  • Applied AI and digital product innovation
  • Prompt engineering and AI-enabled workflows
  • Digital transformation and capacity development
  • Responsible, human-centred technology adoption
